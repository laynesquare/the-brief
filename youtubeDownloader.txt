For yt-dlp:

"YOUTUBE_URL" is the video URL you want to download.
-o source.mp4 means output filename. It tells yt-dlp to save the result as source.mp4.

For FFmpeg:

-ss 00:01:20 means seek/start at 1 minute 20 seconds.
-to 00:01:27 means stop at 1 minute 27 seconds.
-i source.mp4 means input file.
-c copy means copy the existing audio/video streams without re-encoding.
clip.mp4 is the output filename.


yt-dlp https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CkTVoLamPio -o source.mp4

ffmpeg -ss 00:00:37 -to 00:00:50 \
  -i source.mp4 \
  -c copy clip.mp4
