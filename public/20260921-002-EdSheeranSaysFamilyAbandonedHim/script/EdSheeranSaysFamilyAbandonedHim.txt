Ed Sheeran says even his family abandoned him—and the fallout started with his opening act.

Macklemore was dropped from his tour after a pro-Palestinian speech. Reportedly, venues threatened to cancel shows if he stayed.

Then came the personal backlash: Ed’s cousin publicly turned on him.

In Philadelphia, Ed broke down, telling fans he felt abandoned by friends and family.

But he thanked the crowd for showing up.

The tour continued. The damage to those relationships? That’s less clear.

Should Ed have risked the shows to stand by Macklemore?
